# Gazette Voice: things to try (plain language)

For whoever tries the add-on on beta. Each line is something to do and what you should see.

## Reading
1. Open a club Gazette that has Gazette Voice on. The **Newsroom** tab is selected first, next to Easy and Expert.
2. A story has a headline, a grey "from the ... dept." line, "Posted by the ... desk", a paragraph with blue links, a question in bold and small rounded chips under it.
3. Click each blue link. Every one opens a page that is about that paper or its topic (the paper, a news article, a discussion thread, code).
4. A paper nobody has covered says so in one short line and has a chip "No outside coverage yet".
5. Tap **Easy**: the page reads exactly as it did before the add-on. Tap **Newsroom** again.
6. Switch the Gazette to Urdu: the Newsroom story is in Urdu, right to left, with its own pun; links and numbers still read correctly.
7. On a phone, nothing scrolls sideways and the chips wrap onto new lines.

## Terms
8. Some words have a tint and a small tag: Easy, Advanced or Expert. Click one: its term page opens.
9. Hover over a term, or tab to it with the keyboard: a small card shows its one-line explainer and level.
10. Turn off **Highlight terms**: the tinted words become plain text and are no longer links. Turn it back on.
11. Turn off just **Expert**: only the Expert words go plain.
12. Reload the page: your choice is remembered.

## Suggesting a fix (any signed-in member)
13. On a story, tap **Suggest a fix**, pick "Wording or typo", select three words, type a better wording and send. The story changes straight away, and **History** shows your change as "applied automatically".
14. Try a fix that changes a number. It does not apply itself; it says it is waiting for the club's managers.
15. Send a second fix on the same story while the first is waiting: you are told you already have one open.

## Managing (club owner, admin or moderator)
16. A gear icon is on the Gazette masthead. A normal member does not see it.
17. Tap the gear. **Overview** shows this edition's spend against the $0.50 cap and how many stories fell back to plain.
18. **Runs**: this week's English and Urdu runs, with cost. Open one: its calls are listed.
19. **Calls**: open the writer's call. The prompt is split into house rules, the club's voice, learned fixes and the edition's data; the reply is under it, then the checks and the council's verdicts.
20. **Prompts**: edit the English writer voice (add a line), save it as a new version. Tap **Try**, pick two papers: a price is shown before anything runs. Run it: the trial stories appear next to the live ones and are not published. Make it default; roll back to the old one.
21. **Fixes**: the waiting fix from step 14 is there. Choose **Regenerate with it**: a price is shown first; after it runs, the story changes and History says why.
22. Choose **Also learn it** on a fix and write it as one rule: it appears under learned fixes in Prompts.
23. Undo the automatic fix from step 13: the old wording comes back.
